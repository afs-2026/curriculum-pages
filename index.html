Overview

26 October–20 November 2026 · 20 training days

Learning Path

  1. Days 1–5 · Trustworthy Local Change

    Make a small code change understandable, bounded, tested, and reviewable.

  2. Days 6–10 · Vertical Change

    Extend local engineering judgment across UI, API, domain, and data boundaries.

  3. Days 11–13 · AI-Augmented Engineering

    Delegate implementation to AI without delegating engineering accountability.

  4. Days 14–20 · Team Product Delivery

    Discover, plan, deliver, learn from, and defend a validated product increment as a team.

Class Hours

Monday to Friday · China Standard Time (UTC+8). No scheduled classes on weekends.

Morning: 08:40–12:00 (Mondays: 09:00–12:00). Afternoon: 13:30–17:30.

AI Use

“Controlled AI use” marks sessions where AI use is required within limits set by the instructor. Follow the instructor’s instructions for each activity.

Days 1–10

Learners do most engineering work without AI. Only sessions marked Controlled AI use may use it. Day 10 permits bounded assistance for selected test moves and fixed browser-test generation. Learners choose the test mix and expected results, review assertions, decide any test removal, and judge the evidence. AI must not change product code or agreed behavior.

Days 11–20

AI is available throughout appropriate engineering work, subject to scope, permissions, checkpoints, and independent verification.

Learning Goals

Use a concept map to explain ideas and their relationships. Clarify an unclear request with questions and concrete examples. Check an AI-generated change against agreed results.

Learning Focus
Capabilities
  • Behavior Clarification

    Turn ambiguous intent into observable examples, decisions, and acceptance boundaries.

  • Engineering Accountability

    Explain decisions, AI contribution, human judgment, and evidence for an accepted result.

Methods
  • Specification by Example

    Clarify behavior through concrete examples and counterexamples.

  • Given / When / Then

    Express preconditions, events, and observable outcomes.

  • Concept Mapping

    Use concepts and linking words to explain relationships between ideas. Mark uncertain links and revise the map using feedback and evidence.

  • ORID

    Guide a conversation through facts (Objective), feelings (Reflective), meaning (Interpretive), and next actions (Decisional), in that order.

Knowledge
  • Observable Behavior

    Externally visible outcomes of a change.

  • Acceptance Boundary

    Included, excluded, and uncertain behavior.

  • Change Scope

    The in-scope, out-of-scope, and uncertain boundaries of a change.

  • Engineering Lifecycle

    How intent, change, observable behavior, verification, and feedback connect across software delivery.

Technologies

    A constrained AI assistant used for exploration and implementation.
