Learner Guide · v1.0.0

OverviewTimetable

2026 Graduate Engineering Bootcamp

26 October–20 November 2026 · 20 training days

Learning Path

  1. Days 1–5 · Trustworthy Local Change

    Make a small code change understandable, bounded, tested, and reviewable.

  2. Days 6–10 · Vertical Change

    Extend local engineering judgment across UI, API, domain, and data boundaries.

  3. Days 11–13 · AI-Augmented Engineering

    Delegate implementation to AI without delegating engineering accountability.

  4. Days 14–20 · Team Product Delivery

    Discover, plan, deliver, learn from, and defend a validated product increment as a team.

Class Hours

Monday to Friday · China Standard Time (UTC+8). No scheduled classes on weekends.

Morning: 08:40–12:00 (Mondays: 09:00–12:00). Afternoon: 13:30–17:30.

AI Use

“Controlled AI use” marks sessions where AI use is required within limits set by the instructor. Follow the instructor’s instructions for each activity.

Days 1–10

Learners do most engineering work without AI. Only sessions marked Controlled AI use may use it. Day 10 permits bounded assistance for selected test moves and fixed browser-test generation. Learners choose the test mix and expected results, review assertions, decide any test removal, and judge the evidence. AI must not change product code or agreed behavior.

Days 11–20

AI is available throughout appropriate engineering work, subject to scope, permissions, checkpoints, and independent verification.

Timetable

26 October–20 November 2026 · China Standard Time (UTC+8)

Classes run Monday to Friday. Weekends are free of scheduled classes. Mondays start at 09:00; other days start at 08:40. Lunch is 12:00–13:30. Each day ends at 17:30.

All classes use this timetable. Class showcases allow for up to 4 groups. The final showcase brings all 10 teams together; teams present in sequence. Team numbers below show presentation order.
