课程概览

2026 Graduate Engineering Training

从个人可信变更，扩展到跨边界交付、AI 协作与团队产品责任。

20教学日164教学环节15作业

学习路径

Days 1–5

Trustworthy Local Change

Make a small code change understandable, bounded, tested, and reviewable.

阶段检查 Can the learner independently make and explain a trustworthy local change?

Days 6–10

Vertical Change

Extend local engineering judgment across UI, API, domain, and data boundaries.

阶段检查 Can the learner deliver and verify one observable behavior across system boundaries?

Days 11–13

AI-Augmented Engineering

Delegate implementation to AI without delegating engineering accountability.

阶段检查 Can the learner use an established P0 baseline, run P1–P5 with G1–G3, and independently verify an AI-assisted change?

Days 14–20

Team Product Delivery

Discover, plan, deliver, learn from, and defend a validated product increment as a team.

阶段检查 Can the learner contribute identifiable decisions and evidence to a team-delivered increment?

AI 使用政策

Days 1–10 · 默认不使用 AI

Learners do most engineering work without AI; only sessions marked controlled-required may use it.

Establish independent clarification, code reading, testing, debugging, and verification judgment.
Days 11–20 · 使用 AI

AI is available throughout appropriate engineering work, subject to scope, permissions, checkpoints, and independent verification.

Practice the complete AI4SE lifecycle and AI-augmented team delivery.

已确定的结构约束

  • Day 11–13 覆盖 P1→P5 与 G1→G3。
  • Day 16–19 由团队主导项目实践，讲师提供辅导，不预设讲解或演示环节。
  • Day 1–15 有结构化作业；Day 16–19 改为项目延续任务；Day 20 安排彩排与最终展示，不设作业。

evidence led debugging

2025 Debugging was mainly implicit in coding and framework exercises

2026 Day 5 teaches diagnosis, small repairs, and regression checks. Day 9 applies the same method to one bounded failure across the UI, API, and data path.

Learners need to diagnose unexpected behavior independently of AI-generated explanations. Reuse the method across system layers while preserving time to learn the components and requests being checked.

强化

behavior before implementation

2025 User stories and acceptance criteria concentrated in product-design activities

2026 Concrete examples start on Day 1 and recur through tests, specifications, and project work

Faster implementation makes definition quality and observable correctness a central engineering capability.

备注：Day 1 keeps 60 minutes for the course introduction, group introductions, and learning expectations. It teaches concept mapping as a learning tool through 20 minutes of explanation, 40 minutes of individual work and group comparison, and 20 minutes of presentations and feedback. AI coding is the open topic. A 20-minute software change example replaces the longer process overview. Clarification practice includes short demonstrations, confirmed rule sources, individual examples, and feedback. Homework checks one supplied new example; unconfirmed rules remain questions during Day 2 review.

关联教学日：Day 1 · Day 3 · Day 6 · Day 11 · Day 14 · Day 15

ai4se

2025 AI-assisted SDLC topics concentrated near the end of technical training

2026 AI appears in controlled scenarios from Day 1; systematic AI-enabled engineering starts on Day 11 with a prepared P0 baseline and guided P1–P5 practice. Day 12 includes acceptance planning before two bounded implementation rounds. Day 13 adds independent review, repair and recheck, specification consistency, and a second change using the updated baseline

Small changes connect approved behavior, AI tasks, independent evidence, and current specifications. Guided practice and real gate blockers preserve human accountability without long documents.

备注：Day 1 uses a 20-minute AI demonstration to reveal assumptions, followed by 75 minutes of clarification practice without AI and 75 minutes of individual clarification and controlled AI practice. Learners write expected results before asking AI to change code through natural-language instructions. They record versions and actual results for comparison. Prepared environments and runnable backups keep tool delays from taking over the lesson. The final 30 minutes cover evidence, feedback, concept-map revision, and homework.

project rhythm

2025 Project inception followed by repeated stand-up, implementation, review, showcase, and retro

2026 Day 15 team formation, story map, MVP, iteration goal, and P0 inception. Days 16–19 each start with a stand-up and end with a code review. Two iterations include showcases, feedback, and retros. Day 20 closes with final validation, showcase rehearsal, and a team showcase with individual defense, without a separate retro or homework.

Give teams a consistent daily feedback loop while preserving iteration feedback and verification. Use the final day for rehearsal and evidence-based individual accountability.

testing to verification

2025 Unit testing and TDD were taught chiefly as engineering topics

2026 Examples lead to unit, regression, API, integration, E2E, and independent verification evidence

Learners need evidence for risks across system boundaries. Day 10 uses approved behavior and impact analysis to teach check selection before learners implement the same small change.
